What are the key maintenance requirements for attachments?

Regular maintenance includes inspecting wear parts, checking for cracks or damage, and ensuring proper lubrication of moving components. Timely replacement of worn edges, teeth, or tools, along with proper storage and handling, helps maintain performance and extends attachment life.
